Questions before you send the link?

Which platforms do you work with?

We focus first on WordPress, Wix, Webflow and straightforward static sites. We confirm compatibility before accepting implementation work.

Do you need access just to review my homepage?

No. A free review only needs your public website URL. Access is requested only after you approve paid implementation.

Do I need to join a call?

No. The full process can be handled by email. A call is optional if you prefer one.

Do you guarantee more customers or conversions?

No. We improve clarity, usability and contact friction, but we do not promise revenue, leads, rankings or conversion outcomes.

Will you rebuild my whole website?

Not under the focused Homepage Fix offer. The initial scope is one homepage. Larger work can be quoted separately only if it is genuinely needed.

How quickly can it be done?

For a standard scoped homepage fix, the target is 24–48 hours after approval, deposit and required access. Complex platform issues may change that timeline.
